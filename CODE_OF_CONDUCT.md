# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want a professional, harassment-free project for everyone who reports bugs, reviews code, or sends a patch. We do not tolerate harassment, slurs, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the code and the evidence.
- Assume good faith until the record shows otherwise.
- Scan only targets you are authorized to test.
- Keep other people's private data out of issues, pull requests, and commits.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or publication of someone's personal contact details, location, age, or school.
- Submitting exploits against systems you do not have permission to test.
- Opening pull requests that add leaked credentials or fabricated security claims.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or lock issues and pull requests that break this conduct. Report conduct problems through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/ryaneditraj/thugir-scanner/security/advisories/new) if a public issue would expose someone.
